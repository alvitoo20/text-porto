♥ ABOUT ME

About Alvito

Alvito DarrellEN / 日本語 OK!

Alvito is a Motion Graphic Designer, Director, and 3DCG Artist from Indonesia. His main focus is entertainment video — PVs and MVs for VTubers, especially hololive Indonesia talents, as well as hololive EN/JP talents and independent creators.

On foriio, he is listed as a Motion Designer, 3D Designer, and 3D Animator. One of his latest credits: MV Producer for “CLOSE(R)”, Vestia Zeta's 3rd original song released on March 25, 2026 for holoh3ro's 4th anniversary (together with CissXD).

Every project is treated like the song itself: a sweet intro, an explosive chorus, and tiny details that make viewers smile. If you have a song or event that needs visuals with that same energy — let's talk!

♪ HOW I WORK

How we'll work together

1

Brief & Chat

Tell me about your song, concept, references, and deadline via email or X DM. We'll align on the vision until it's crystal clear.

2

Concept & Styleframe

I'll develop the visual direction: moodboard, styleframes, and a rough storyboard so you can picture the final result.

3

Production

Animation & compositing stage. You'll receive regular previews to make sure everything stays on track.

4

Revision & Delivery

Polishing based on your feedback, then the final file is delivered ready to upload. Time to release — enjoy! ✦
